How we label the missing stopping reasons

Both methods start with the same trials and finish with five reason predictions. The difference is which missing labels they recover, and how those recovered labels train the final classifiers.

Teammates’ selected hybrid

10 clusters

The final classifiers also use the original 150 scaled embeddings.

  1. Cluster the scaled embeddingsK-means: 10 clusters in the original 150 dimensions.
  2. Estimate cluster reason ratesSmooth rates toward the observed-label frequencies.
  3. Blend for all five reasons50% smoothed cluster rate + 50% vote from 3 originally labeled neighbors.
  4. Recover every masked row30% cutoff. If no reason is positive, choose the highest-scoring reason. All 1,014 rows receive five labels; zero remain unresolved.
  5. Train five logistic regressionsC=0.1; recovered rows get weight 0.25.

Recent controlled experiments on the 14-cluster model

Each row starts from the model above it or from the stated Safety-only reference. These are validation results only; no CSV or Kaggle submission was made for the Safety-only or Efficacy/Recruitment fallback experiments.

1. Regularize Safety only

Change only Safety’s logistic-regression C from 1 to 0.1. Funding, Efficacy, Recruitment, and Other retain the exact base classifiers and final decisions. Safety F1 rises from 0.7059 to 0.8889; sample F1 rises to 0.9231 and macro F1 to 0.8291.

2. Fill only missing Efficacy and Recruitment labels

Two separate trials start from Safety-only C=0.1. Each fills the same 520 unresolved training rows for Efficacy and Recruitment, then refits only those two classifiers. Safety, Funding, and Other final decisions stay fixed.

Cluster argmax fallback: All 520 missing E/R training rows were assigned Recruitment; none were assigned Efficacy. Efficacy and Recruitment validation F1 both fell sharply.

Three-neighbor fallback: Among those same 520 training rows, 142 received Efficacy=1 and 364 received Recruitment=1. The two reasons are independent, so a row can receive both. Their validation F1 also fell relative to the Safety-only reference, although less than with cluster argmax.

These “520 fallback rows” are missing training labels for Efficacy and Recruitment. They are unrelated to the rule that gives an otherwise empty test prediction a nonempty reason.
